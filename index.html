Suggested initial ventilator settings

Respiratory pressures (Pinsp & PEEP)
  • PIP 15–30 (aim for chest wall movement / Vt)
  • PEEP 5–8 (always have on)
Respiratory rate
  • <10 kg: 25–30
  • >20 kg: 20–25
  • >50 kg: 12–20
Tidal volume
  • 5–8 ml/kg, aim for chest rise
Difficult to ventilate?
  • Has ETT moved / kinked?
  • Aspirate NG
  • Consider ETT saline + physio
Hamilton ventilator: use the smaller circuit if <15 kg (from the checklist).
